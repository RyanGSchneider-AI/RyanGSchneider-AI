## 1. How to Use This Map

This document serves different audiences in different ways:

| Audience | Primary Use |
|---|---|
| **Executive Leadership** | Identify decision owners, gaps in coverage, and organizational dependencies |
| **Product / Engineering** | Determine who to consult, inform, and get sign-off from during development |
| **Sales / BD** | Understand internal champions, blockers, and budget owners for deals |
| **HR / People Ops** | Understand team structure, reporting lines, and cross-functional relationships |
| **Any Initiative Owner** | Use RACI (Section 3) to assign roles before work begins |

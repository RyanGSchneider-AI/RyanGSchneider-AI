## 1. How to Classify Key Customers

> Not every customer belongs in this document — only those with strategic importance
> beyond standard commercial terms. Use the criteria below to determine inclusion.

**Include if the account meets one or more of the following:**

- **Revenue**: Top revenue accounts by segment or overall
- **Reference Value**: Willing to serve as a public reference, case study, or testimonial
- **Co-Development**: Active participant in product direction, beta programs, or feature development
- **Pilot**: First adopter of a new product, feature, or market expansion
- **Strategic Foothold**: Provides access to a new segment, geography, or distribution channel
